I earned my M.S. in Computer Science from Georgia Tech in May 2026. My research at SAIL focused on federated learning, distributed training optimization, and efficient inference for large-scale machine learning systems. I will be joining Google in July 2026.

I am passionate about building fault-tolerant data platforms and scalable infrastructure for real-world AI applications. Prior to graduate school, I worked as a Senior Software Engineer at PhonePe, where I designed and scaled high-throughput distributed systems. My work included building a strongly consistent schema registry, improving an OLAP datastore, and scaling the event ingestion gateway to process over 1.6 million messages per second.
