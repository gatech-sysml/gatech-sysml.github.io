Previously, I worked on environmental deep learning under the supervision of Dr. Albert Bifet. I contributed to Catalonia's emergency COVID-19 response, where I analyzed and modeled data under the supervision of Dr. Clara Prats. I've also worked as a financial data scientist and was briefly a research visitor at the Barcelona Supercomputing Centre.

I received a BSc in Data Science and an MSc in Informatics Engineering from the Polytechnic University of Catalonia.
